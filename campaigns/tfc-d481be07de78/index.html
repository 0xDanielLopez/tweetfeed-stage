About this campaign

A ClickFix and ClearFake social engineering campaign uses fake landing pages on getpixeltap.com and siege-close.com, plus a Cloudflare R2 bucket and beer-TLD CDN domains, to deliver CobaltStrike malware. A C2 IP at 31.76.87.37 responds on ports 80 and 8443, and multiple hashes are known. Seven researchers reported 54 IOCs between 2026-08-07 and 2026-08-11.
