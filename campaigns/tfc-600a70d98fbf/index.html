About this campaign

Fifty UUID-pattern subdomains on b-cdn.net, BunnyCDN's infrastructure domain, are tagged as phishing hosts. The UUID naming convention matches BunnyCDN pull-zone or push-zone assignments, suggesting attackers are abusing the platform's shared domain to host phishing content behind CDN infrastructure.
